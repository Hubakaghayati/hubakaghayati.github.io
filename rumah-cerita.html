The Story Behind The Song

RUMAH CERITA

A song about memories, time, love, and the stories that remain.

Why I Made This Song

There are moments in life when we suddenly stop and look back. Not because we want to return, but because we realize how many stories have quietly shaped who we are today.

"Rumah Cerita" was born from that feeling — the feeling of remembering the people, places, conversations, and moments that once filled our days. Some are still close. Some have gone away. And some exist only in memory.

What This Song Is About

Time keeps moving, but not everything disappears with it. There are memories that remain quietly inside us, even after the people and places connected to them have changed.

This song is about those memories. About love that once existed. About people who once walked beside us. And about the stories that continue to live inside our hearts.

What if every memory we carry
is a room inside the house of our lives?

The Meaning of "Rumah Cerita"

A house is not only made of walls. Sometimes, a house is made of voices, laughter, silence, photographs, footsteps, and memories.

That is what "Rumah Cerita" means to me. A place within ourselves where every story we have lived can still find a home.

Some stories may never return. Some people may never come back. But what they left behind can still become part of our journey.

How the Song Was Created

The song began with a simple thought: one day, our journey will come to an end.

What will remain then? Perhaps not the things we collected, but the stories we shared, the people we loved, and the moments that became part of us.

Some stories end.
But some stories become part of who we are.

Rumah cerita itu tidak selalu berupa tempat.
Kadang, ia hidup di dalam hati.
