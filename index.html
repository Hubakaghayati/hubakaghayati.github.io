The Story Behind the Song

ARE YOU STILL HUMAN?

“Are You Still Human?” was born from a simple question that became increasingly difficult to ignore: what happens to our humanity when we become accustomed to seeing the suffering of others?

Why I Made This Song

This song grew out of a quiet uneasiness about the world around us. We live in an age of extraordinary technology. We can communicate across continents in seconds, witness events happening thousands of miles away, and have more information than any generation before us.

Yet sometimes, the more connected we become, the easier it seems to become disconnected from the feelings of others.

What I Saw and Felt

I saw suffering, violence, indifference, greed, and division becoming things that people could witness every day and then simply scroll past. A tragedy appears on a screen, we watch it for a moment, and then move on to the next story.

It made me wonder: Are we still able to stop for a moment and truly feel?

Are empathy, compassion, and kindness still strong enough to guide us when the world becomes increasingly noisy and fast?

What Does “Are You Still Human?” Mean?

To me, “Are You Still Human?” is not a question about whether we are human physically. It is a question about what remains inside us that makes us human.

When we see suffering but choose not to care. When we know something is wrong but remain silent. When our own interests become more important than another person's life. When another person's tears no longer move our hearts.

At those moments, perhaps we need to stop and ask ourselves:

“Are You Still Human?”

And that question is not only for others. It is also for me. For all of us.

The Message

I did not want this song to simply be a song about sadness or anger. I wanted it to become a mirror — an invitation to look back at ourselves.

Being human is not only about intelligence, technology, achievement, or success. It is also about being able to feel another person's pain, showing empathy, offering a helping hand, forgiving, loving, and choosing not to hurt when we have the power to do so.

Perhaps we cannot change the whole world by ourselves. But we can begin with something very small:

“Do not let your heart lose its ability to feel.”

How the Song Was Created

“Are You Still Human?” began with a question and a feeling of uneasiness, rather than a desire to simply create a beautiful song.

I tried to translate that feeling into lyrics and music. Because the message is strong, I wanted the music to leave enough space for the words and the questions in the lyrics to be heard.

AI technology became part of the creative process, helping me transform the idea into music. I then shaped and refined the result so that it could better represent the message I wanted to share.

For me, technology is only a tool. The most important things are still the idea, the feeling, and the message behind the song.

And perhaps there is a small irony in that.

I used modern technology to create a song that asks us to return to something very old and deeply human:

Are we still capable of having a heart?

Are You Still Human?
