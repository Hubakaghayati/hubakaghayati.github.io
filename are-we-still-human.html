The Story Behind the Song

ARE WE STILL HUMAN?

A song about humanity, suffering, and the question we sometimes avoid.

Why I Made This Song

“Are We Still Human?” was born from a question I could no longer ignore: what happens to our humanity when we become accustomed to the suffering of others?

What I Saw and Felt

We live in a world where technology allows us to see everything, everywhere, almost instantly. Yet sometimes, the more connected we become, the more distant we seem to feel.

I saw violence, suffering, indifference, and division becoming things we could watch on a screen and simply scroll past.

Are we still able to stop and truly feel?

What This Song Is About

I did not make this song to tell people what to think. I made it as a mirror.

Perhaps we cannot change the whole world ourselves. But we can choose not to let our hearts become numb.

Do not let your heart lose its ability to feel.

How the Song Was Created

The song began with a feeling of uneasiness and a simple question. I wanted the music to leave enough space for the words and the message to breathe.

The feeling, the question, and the message are still human.
